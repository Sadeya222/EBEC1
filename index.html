Current Assessment of High School Student Dual Enrollment

expand_more

This comprehensive report examines the current state of dual enrollment programs across the nation, analyzing participation rates, student outcomes, and institutional practices. Our findings reveal significant disparities in access and success, particularly for underrepresented student populations. The report provides actionable recommendations for improving program quality and equity.

Effects of Dual Enrollment Programs on Colleges

expand_more

This study explores the multifaceted impact of dual enrollment programs on higher education institutions. From enrollment management and curriculum alignment to student success metrics and resource allocation, we analyze how these programs influence college operations and outcomes. The research highlights best practices for partnerships between high schools and colleges that benefit both institutions and students.

Dual Enrollment Programs and Education Equality

expand_more

This report critically examines the role of dual enrollment programs in promoting or hindering educational equity. Through quantitative analysis and case studies, we identify systemic barriers that limit access for marginalized student populations and highlight program models that successfully bridge opportunity gaps. The findings provide a framework for designing more inclusive dual enrollment initiatives that serve all students equitably.
